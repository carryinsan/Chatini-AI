01

What this agreement covers

These ArixAI Beta Tester Program Terms (“Agreement”) apply when you accept an invitation to test pre-release, trial, experimental, or specially enabled ArixAI features (“Program”). They are separate from ArixAI's general Terms of Service and Privacy Notice. Those documents continue to apply to your ordinary account and use of ArixAI.

This Agreement adds the rules and consent choices that specifically relate to testing. It does not remove rights that cannot legally be waived. If a term conflicts with mandatory law, mandatory law controls. If there is a conflict with the general ArixAI terms, this Agreement controls only for the specific tester-program subject it addresses, unless the law requires otherwise.

02

Eligibility and permission

  • You must have received a genuine ArixAI tester invitation and provide accurate information reasonably needed to administer the Program.
  • You must be legally able to accept this Agreement. If you are under 18, do not join unless your parent or legal guardian has reviewed and agreed through the verified process ArixAI specifies, and ArixAI has confirmed your participation. A simple click by a minor is not a substitute for any legally required verified guardian consent.
  • You must have authority to submit any material you upload. Do not test with another person's account or use data you are not authorised to share.
  • Participation is voluntary. You may decline without being required to explain why; declining this Program does not itself cancel your ordinary ArixAI account.
03

Complimentary paid-plan access

In exchange for participating in testing and providing honest feedback, ArixAI may enable paid-plan features for a limited period (“Tester Access”). The specific plan, start date, expiry date, feature limits and any special instructions will be stated in your invitation or confirmed in writing by ArixAI.

  • Tester Access is complimentary, temporary, non-transferable, and has no cash value. It is not a promise of a permanent subscription, lifetime access, or a future discount.
  • Tester Access expires on the stated end date unless ArixAI confirms an extension in writing. It does not automatically convert to a paid subscription, and ArixAI must not charge you for it merely because the test period ends.
  • You do not need to provide a payment card solely to receive Tester Access. Any later paid purchase must be a separate, clearly presented choice.
  • Normal plan limits, fair-use controls, safety rules and provider availability continue to apply. ArixAI may temporarily limit a feature when reasonably necessary for security, reliability, cost control, or testing.
  • Unless separately agreed in writing, the benefit is access to ArixAI features, not cash compensation, wages, employment, or guaranteed rewards.

Duration: Your invitation sets the actual testing period. This Agreement does not invent a default number of free days.

04

Experimental features and test risks

Tester features may be incomplete, change without notice, produce incorrect or inconsistent results, lose work, experience outages, or be withdrawn. Features and usage limits may differ from the standard paid plan. A feature that works during one test may not remain available.

AI responses can be inaccurate, outdated, biased, incomplete, or unsafe. Independently verify important information before relying on it. Do not use a test result as the sole basis for medical, legal, financial, safety-critical, employment, educational assessment, or other high-impact decisions. Keep your own backup of anything important.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, pre-release features are provided on an “as available” and experimental basis. Nothing in this section removes a consumer or statutory right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

05

What tester data may be processed

Depending on the features you use and the information you choose to provide, ArixAI may process the following categories (“Tester Data”):

CategoryExamplesWhy it is needed
Account and program dataAccount ID, contact details, plan, invitation status, consent choicesTo manage access, eligibility, communications and your choices
Test interactionsPrompts, conversation context, uploaded files/images, generated responses, edits and ratingsTo reproduce issues, evaluate outputs and improve tested features
Feedback and reportsBug reports, surveys, suggestions, feature requests and support messagesTo prioritise fixes and understand tester experience

Technical logs may sometimes contain snippets of submitted content if the relevant service or error trace captures them; ArixAI should configure logging to avoid unnecessary prompt or file contents wherever practicable.

06

How Tester Data may improve ArixAI

By joining the Program and giving the specific consent in the enrolment flow, you authorise ArixAI to process Tester Data for these defined purposes:

  • Evaluate answer quality, usefulness, accuracy, latency, cost, reliability, safety and user experience.
  • Find bugs, reproduce failures, fix code, tune prompts and workflows, improve retrieval and tool use, and design or assess features.
  • Prepare test cases, quality benchmarks, error categories, and aggregated or de-identified analysis to measure improvements over time.
  • Allow appropriately authorised team members or reviewers, subject to confidentiality and access controls, to inspect relevant test material when reasonably necessary for evaluation, debugging, safety review or support.

Model training is a separate choice. Using content to evaluate or improve a product does not automatically mean it will be used to train or fine-tune a model. ArixAI may use selected Tester Data for direct training or fine-tuning of models under ArixAI's control only if you separately opt in to that use in the consent flow. The optional choice should not be pre-selected.

If you opt in to model training, selected content may be filtered, redacted or de-identified where reasonably feasible before use. However, model training can produce model parameters or derived artefacts from which an individual contribution may not be readily identifiable or removable. Please do not opt in—or submit the material—if that possibility is unacceptable to you.

ArixAI may retain and use properly aggregated or de-identified statistics for longer-term product analysis where they no longer identify you and are not reasonably usable to identify you. ArixAI should not intentionally publish your identifiable prompts, files, conversations, or account identity without separate permission, unless disclosure is required or permitted by law.

No blanket ownership transfer: Submitting Tester Data does not transfer ownership of your original content to ArixAI. You grant only the limited processing permission described here and any separate model-training permission you choose to give.

07

Hosting, AI vendors and other service providers

ArixAI may use external service providers for hosting, storage, security, model inference, web search, OCR, analytics, email or error monitoring. When a feature needs one of these providers, relevant portions of your input or file may be sent to that provider to deliver the feature, maintain security or troubleshoot a problem.

Those providers may process data under their own applicable contracts, privacy notices, retention practices and technical settings. ArixAI should select and configure providers responsibly and restrict provider training or secondary use where a suitable control is available. Unless ArixAI expressly confirms it in its current Privacy Notice, do not assume that every provider has zero retention, processes data only in India, or can immediately delete all copies.

Some providers or infrastructure may operate outside your state or country. The actual vendors, locations and safeguards must be described in the applicable Privacy Notice or other notice given at enrolment. Do not use the Program for content whose confidentiality requirements prohibit this processing.

08

Your privacy choices and requests

You can stop participating at any time and withdraw consent for future optional processing by using the official contact channel below or the channel through which you were invited. ArixAI should make withdrawal reasonably straightforward and should not require you to justify it.

After a valid withdrawal or deletion request, ArixAI will stop the relevant future processing and take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise identifiable Tester Data under its control, subject to applicable law and legitimate security or recordkeeping needs. Some information may remain temporarily in protected backups, security logs, records needed to resolve a dispute or comply with law, or with service providers under their retention rules. Properly de-identified aggregate statistics may not be traceable back to you.

Content already used in a model-training run with your separate opt-in may not be individually removable from resulting model parameters. That limitation does not remove any right that applicable law gives you. Contact ArixAI promptly if you want to withdraw before your data is used for a training run.

Data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes disclosed in this Agreement, the applicable Privacy Notice, and legal or security needs. The operator must set and follow a concrete retention schedule for raw chats, uploads, logs, backups and provider copies; do not publish a retention promise that the actual systems cannot meet.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access information about your personal data, correct it, request deletion, withdraw consent, or raise a complaint. Requests should be sent to the privacy contact listed below. The applicable Privacy Notice explains the full process.

09

Your responsibilities as a tester

  • Use ArixAI lawfully and honestly, and report actual results—including failures. Do not invent test outcomes or submit intentionally misleading feedback.
  • Use synthetic or redacted examples. Do not submit passwords, one-time passcodes, API keys, financial credentials, government identity numbers, sensitive personal data, private health information, or confidential information belonging to another person or organisation.
  • Do not share your account, tokens, tester invitation, or non-public access with another person unless ArixAI authorises it.
  • Do not attempt unauthorised access, steal or expose other users' data, bypass security or usage controls, interfere with the service, overload systems, or test production systems beyond the scope ArixAI has explicitly authorised.
  • Respect intellectual-property rights and obtain permission before uploading third-party materials. Do not use the Program to create unlawful, abusive, deceptive or harmful material.
  • Tell ArixAI if you believe your account or test content has been exposed, or if you accidentally submitted sensitive information.
10

Confidentiality and public comments

Some invitations may expose non-public features, interface designs, roadmaps, test credentials, internal documentation or unreleased results. Unless ArixAI has made them public or has given permission, keep such non-public material confidential and use it only for the Program. Report security issues privately through the agreed channel rather than publishing exploit details or other users' data.

This section does not prevent you from making truthful statements about your own experience with publicly available features, reporting suspected unlawful conduct to an appropriate authority, obtaining independent advice, or exercising a legal right that cannot be restricted by contract.

11

Feedback, suggestions and intellectual property

You retain rights you already own in your original content, subject to the limited permissions you give for processing under this Agreement. You must have the right to provide any material you submit.

If you voluntarily send suggestions, ideas, ratings or recommendations specifically about ArixAI (“Feedback”), you grant ArixAI a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free right to use, reproduce, adapt and implement that Feedback to develop, test, maintain and improve ArixAI, without owing you payment or attribution. This does not grant ArixAI ownership of your unrelated work or permission to disclose your identity publicly.

ArixAI retains its rights in its existing services, code, designs, systems, trademarks and independently developed improvements, subject to your rights and applicable law. Nothing in this clause grants ArixAI a licence to use your submitted conversations for direct model training unless you separately opt in as described in Section 06.

12

Security and responsible disclosure

ArixAI should use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data and the stage of the service, but no internet service can promise absolute security. You are responsible for safeguarding your own device, account and credentials.

If you discover a vulnerability, stop testing the affected path once you have enough evidence to report it safely. Do not access, alter, retain or disclose another person's data, and do not exploit the issue further without written authorisation. Send a concise report using the privacy/support contact or the private reporting channel provided in your invitation.

13

Withdrawal, suspension and end of access

You may leave the Program at any time. ArixAI may end or pause your Tester Access or the Program when reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, technical issues, a change in product direction, or completion of the test. Where practical, ArixAI will provide notice, especially when access ends earlier than the invitation stated.

When the stated test period ends or participation stops, complimentary features may be disabled. This does not by itself create a payment obligation. Provisions that reasonably need to continue—such as confidentiality for non-public information, rights in Feedback already granted, lawful recordkeeping, and the handling of already de-identified data—may continue to the extent permitted by law.
